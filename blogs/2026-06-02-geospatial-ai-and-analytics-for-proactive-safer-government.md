State and local government agencies struggle with slow data access and siloed systems that delay critical decisions. Three converging trends — remote sensors, hyper-localization, and generative AI — now enable predictive rather than reactive governance. CARTO's agentic GIS platform lets non-technical users explore spatial data through natural language queries, transforming transportation planning, emergency response, and public safety workflows.
